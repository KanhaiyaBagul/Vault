1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This Agreement governs the confidential acquisition and corporate merger between Apex Holdings and Titan Technologies Inc. Under the terms of this deal, Apex Holdings will acquire 100% of Titan outstanding shareholder equity for a total valuation of $4.8 Billion USD.

2. SHAREHOLDER DIVIDENDS & TREASURY DISBURSEMENT
All preferred shareholders will receive a cash dividend of $42.50 per share at closing. The corporate treasury department has completed the solvency and liquidity compliance audit to ensure seamless disbursement across international jurisdictions.

3. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & ANTITRUST REVIEW
Both parties have submitted the joint antitrust notification to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and the European Commission. Independent legal counsel has verified compliance with SEC disclosures, GDPR data governance, and international cross-border financial regulations.

4. CLOSING TIMELINE & GOVERNANCE
The preliminary closing date is scheduled for December 15, 2026, subject to regulatory antitrust approval and shareholder majority vote.
